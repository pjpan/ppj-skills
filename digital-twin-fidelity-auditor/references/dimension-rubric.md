# Replication Dimension Rubric

Use this file for detailed interpretation of Layer One dimensions.

## 1. 表征复刻

### 人格特质
Relatively stable tendencies in thinking, feeling, and behavior.
Strong coverage needs behavioral implications and repeated evidence, not merely adjectives or type labels.

### 价值观
Long-term priorities over outcomes, goals, and principles.
Inspect:
- priority structure;
- conflicts;
- hard boundaries;
- observed decision evidence;
- contextual modulation.

### 世界观
Relatively stable assumptions about people, organizations, reality, knowledge, change, and uncertainty.

Keep distinct:
- values = what matters;
- problem representation = what is happening here;
- world model = what happens after an action.

### 个人记忆
Accumulated information and experience.
Distinguish:
- semantic memory;
- episodic experience;
- relationship history;
- decision history;
- reflection/learning.

A biography summary is not a temporal memory system.

## 2. 认知复刻

### 当前状态
Current resources, constraints, relationships, pressures, role, objectives, and relevant state.
Without this, systems tend to reproduce a “typical person” rather than the person at a specific moment.

### 问题表征
How the person interprets what the situation fundamentally is.
Inspect entities, relationships, key variables, framing, and ignored factors.

### 世界模型
How the person expects states to change and actions to produce consequences.
Inspect causal beliefs, action→consequence expectations, time horizon, confidence, and domain limits.

### 关键因素选择
Which information the person considers decision-relevant, and its relative importance.

## 3. 决策复刻

### 当前目标
What the person is optimizing now.
Distinguish long-term mission from current-task goal.

### 偏好 / 效用
How competing outcomes are ranked.
Conflict cases carry more information than isolated preference statements.

### 风险 / 约束
What downside is tolerable, unacceptable, reversible, or fatal.
Strong replication is context-dependent rather than one permanent risk score.

### 推理策略
How the person searches, decomposes, compares, eliminates, and stops.
Inspect search order, heuristics, thresholds, elimination, stopping, and counterfactual checks.

Generated chain-of-thought is not proof of human reasoning fidelity.

### 决策策略
How subjective state turns into ranking/choice.
Named heuristics are not sufficient evidence of decision fidelity.

### 拒绝 / 升级策略
When the person would defer, gather more information, seek another person, or refuse to decide.

## 4. 表达复刻

Inspect:
- vocabulary;
- syntax;
- tone;
- structure;
- rhythm;
- framing;
- humor;
- audience/channel adaptation.

Style must be evaluated independently from decision fidelity.

## 5. 行为复刻

For action-capable twins inspect:
- task decomposition;
- tool sequence;
- action sequence;
- delegation;
- persistence;
- stopping;
- external commitments.

Behavioral similarity is meaningful only under matched constraints and comparable information.

## 6. 元认知

Inspect:
- uncertainty monitoring;
- evidence sufficiency;
- contradiction detection;
- information seeking;
- replanning;
- abstention;
- escalation.

Distinguish generic safety guardrails from target-person-specific metacognition.

## 7. 持续学习

Inspect whether new target-person evidence can update:
- memory;
- beliefs/world model;
- preference/risk;
- decision strategy;
- expression.

Track provenance:
- human;
- agent-generated;
- external.

Agent output must not silently become evidence of the human state.

## 8. 安全与授权

If delegation is claimed inspect:
- permission scope;
- action classes;
- risk tiers;
- approval;
- revocation;
- audit trail;
- identity-sensitive commitments.

Predicting a person does not authorize acting as the person.
